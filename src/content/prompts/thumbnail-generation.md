Medium and use: a flat risograph-style illustration, used as the thumbnail of a
blog article. It appears in a list at the size of a fingernail and on the
article page at full width.

Background: a solid #F1F3EA field, a pale warm off-white with a faint green
tint, filling the frame behind the subject. Use this exact color, and no other
color, behind the subject.

Subject: {CONCEPT}, drawn as one clean, original mark, centered with generous
padding.

Key details:

- Geometric construction: the mark is built with compass and straightedge, in
  the manner of Bauhaus geometric abstraction and of Hokusai's ruler-and-compass
  drawing method. Every form is an exact primitive — a true circle, a half or
  quarter of one, a square, a rectangle, a triangle — assembled into the
  subject. Every curve is an arc of a circle drawn at a constant radius, and
  every straight edge runs true, at a right angle or a diagonal of forty-five
  degrees.
- Strong shape: one silhouette built from a few large forms, scalable across
  sizes and still legible as a fingernail-sized thumbnail.
- Balanced negative space: background around every side of the mark, and
  openings inside it wide enough to survive being scaled down.
- Flat design, minimal strokes: one solid, even area of ink per part, with long
  edges and wide curves. Where forms meet, they meet cleanly: an edge lands on
  an edge, a corner rounds on the arc of a circle, and the radius stays the
  same across the mark.
- Broad forms: every part of the mark carries width, and the narrowest of them
  is still a visible band once the image is a fingernail-sized thumbnail.
- Two or three foreground ink colors, harmonious in saturation and brightness,
  reading as one risograph palette. The background does not count toward that
  limit.
- Risograph print texture: halftone grain, paper tooth, visible ink.

Constraints:

- No text, letters, numbers, logo, or watermark.
- One subject only, no scenery, and nothing scattered around the mark.
- No thin lines, and no part that tapers to a point.
- No interior line work, hatching, tick marks, or rows of small shapes.
- No shading, highlight, or tonal variation within a part.
- No drawn outline, stroke, or border around the mark.
- No freehand, sketched, or hand-drawn contours, and no wavering, lumpy, or
  organic outlines. A circle is a full circle rather than a rounded blob.
- No gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no photography.
